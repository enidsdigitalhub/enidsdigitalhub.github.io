# Home Style Quiz

A 12-question quiz that matches visitors to one of 16 interior styles and shows their 9-colour palette with HEX codes, key pieces and what to avoid.

- Built from `style-library/` with `python3 style-library/build_quiz.py apps/style-quiz`. Do not edit `index.html` by hand.
- To link results to your Payhip products, paste the product URLs into the `SHOP` object at the top of the script.
- Once merged to `main`, it is live at `https://enidsdigitalhub.github.io/apps/style-quiz/`.
- Any style can be linked directly, e.g. `/apps/style-quiz/#japandi`.
