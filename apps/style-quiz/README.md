# Home Style Quiz

An 8-question quiz that matches visitors to one of four interior styles (Warm Modern Organic, Highland Stone Lodge, Olive & Terracotta, Terracotta & Beige) and shows their 9-colour palette with HEX codes, key pieces and what to avoid.

- Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step.
- To link results to your Payhip products, paste the product URLs into the `SHOP` object at the top of the script.
- Once merged to `main`, it is live at `https://enidsdigitalhub.github.io/apps/style-quiz/`.
