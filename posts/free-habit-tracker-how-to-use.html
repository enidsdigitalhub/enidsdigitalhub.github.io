How to use a free printable habit tracker (step by step)

A habit tracker only works if it's easy to use on a busy day. The Gentle Habit Tracker is a free one-page monthly tracker for up to 12 habits across 31 days, with space to write why your habits matter and to reflect at the end of the month. You can print it or fill it in on your phone or computer. Here's exactly how to use it.

The 3 steps

  1. Write down 3 small habits. There's room for 12, but start with three you can do even on a tired day, like "Drink water", "Read 10 pages" or "Walk 15 min". Add the month at the top.
  2. Tick a circle each day you show up. Each row has 31 circles, one for every day of the month. Fill in the circle for that day whenever you do the habit. Watching a row fill up is what keeps you going.
  3. Write why it matters to you. Use the "Why these habits matter to me" box for one honest sentence, such as "To feel calmer and have more energy." On hard days, that line will remind you why you started.

Missed a day? That's part of the rhythm

You will have empty circles, and that's fine. The tracker isn't a score. It shows you a pattern. A good rule is to try not to miss two days in a row. One gap is just life; two in a row is when a habit starts to slip.

Printing it vs. filling it in on screen

To print: open the PDF and print page 1 at "Actual size" or 100%. Print a new copy at the start of each month and keep it somewhere you'll see it, like the fridge, your desk or inside your planner.

To fill it in on screen: open the PDF in a free PDF app that supports forms, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader on your phone or computer. Tap a habit line to type, and tap a circle to tick it. Save the file after each update so your ticks are kept.

10 easy habits to start with

  • Drink a glass of water when you wake up
  • Read 10 pages
  • Walk for 15 minutes
  • Plan tomorrow's top 3 tasks
  • No phone for the first 30 minutes
  • Stretch for 5 minutes
  • Write one thing you're grateful for
  • Tidy one small area for 10 minutes
  • Prepare tomorrow's lunch
  • In bed by a set time

At the end of the month

Use the End-of-month reflection box to ask yourself: What helped? What will I keep, change or let go? Then print a fresh copy and carry your best habits into the next month. Swap out the ones that didn't fit your life.
